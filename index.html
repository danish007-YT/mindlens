🎙️ Voice

Optional — only used to set a sensible pitch baseline for the voice check.
Not started

Record 15–20 seconds describing your day, in your natural voice and pace, in English. MindLens listens to what you say (e.g. "today I am very happy") using speech recognition, and scores the meaning of your words — the same understanding used for the text check-in — with acoustic tone as a backup signal if speech isn't picked up clearly.

00:00

Allow microphone access when prompted. If the recording does not start, check your browser's site permissions and reload the page. Speech recognition works best in Chrome/Edge; other browsers fall back to acoustic-only scoring.

Prefer to upload instead? Any audio file works (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, webm…). It's decoded and scored only in your browser — speech recognition isn't run on uploaded files, so tone/pace is used for the score.

🎥 Face & Eyes

Not started

Take a photo with your camera, or upload a photo or short video from your device. A deep convolutional network reads your facial expression from a single frame — processed only in your browser.

For the clearest reading: face a light source, look straight at the camera, and make sure your whole face is visible and in focus.
